Product Label Creator

Barcode

The barcode value is the record key, so it is needed even when the barcode is not printed. Auto picks EAN-13 for 13 digits, UPC-A for 12 digits and Code 128 for anything else. EAN-13 and UPC-A check the check digit, or add it if you leave it out. Print the PNG at 100% (actual size) on a laser printer, then test-scan it.
Print on label

Records

Records are saved in this browser and keyed by the barcode value. Tap a saved record (or type its barcode in the field above) and press Load. Add, Change and Delete are at the bottom of the form. Export a backup now and then — clearing the browser's site data removes saved records. Importing merges with existing records; the same barcode is overwritten by the file.

Save

Add saves a new record keyed by the barcode value. Change overwrites the selected record (if you edited the barcode, the record is re-keyed to the new value). Delete asks for a second tap to confirm.
